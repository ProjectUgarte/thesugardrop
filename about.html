Meet Alison

Hi, I’m Alison

Hi! My name is Alison Ugarte, and I am a self-taught home baker and the owner of The Sugar Drop Custom Confections. I started baking and designing cakes as a hobby, and eventually it led me to start my own business. My goal as a designer and artist is to work collaboratively with my clients to design beautiful custom confections for any occasion.

My cakes are baked fresh and from scratch, never frozen and never from a box. I use only the freshest and highest quality ingredients to ensure your cake tastes delicious.

I take pride in making quality cakes that not only look good but taste good too!

I hope you feel inspired by my cake designs, and I look forward to working with you to create the cake of your dreams.

— Alison
